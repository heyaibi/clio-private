02

Cross-bank subject erasure desynchronization: unpartitioned key_exists check allows global DEK shredding from empty bank without purging derived structures in target banks

Important
Why it matters
When subject_id exists in bank A and erase_request is executed in bank B, the key_exists check succeeds on the unpartitioned subject_keys table. The DEK is destroyed globally, but derived structures (embeddings, FTS, edges, MemTree) in bank A are never purged.
Impact if unaddressed
Derived index data and summaries continue describing personal data after legal crypto-shredding, violating FR-19 / GDPR Art. 17 guarantees.
Evidence
crates/clio-store/src/sqlite_erase.rs:71-86, crates/clio-store/src/postgres_erase.rs:69-82, and sql/001_core.sql:592.
Confidence
High

Proposed fix (not yet applied)

Ensure bank isolation by checking that items exist within the requested bank before proceeding, or fan out derived structure purges across all banks containing the subject.
